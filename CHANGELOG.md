# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-19

First public release.

- Home screen with clock, date, Favourites, Recent and an A–Z app grid
- App menu (hold OK or press Menu): open, pin, move, rename, hide, app info, uninstall
- "+ Add" tile to pin any app to Favourites
- Top bar: notifications with a count badge, voice search, input switcher (HDMI/AV), settings
- Alerts for no network, Wi‑Fi without internet, sign-in required, and low or full storage
- Customize: six background themes, 4–6 apps per row, app names always or on focus,
  sort by A–Z / most used / newest, Recent row on/off, 12/24-hour clock, reset
- Compact pop-up menus anchored to the button or app that opened them
- Settings apps are reachable from the gear instead of being duplicated in the grid
- TV tools: backup, reversible debloat profile, and one-step restore
