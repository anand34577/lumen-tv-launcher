# Using Lumen

Everything in Lumen works with the D-pad, **OK** and **Back**. There's nothing you need a touchscreen,
mouse or keyboard for, apart from typing a new name when you rename an app.

## The home screen

From top to bottom:

1. **Clock and date**, and any [alerts](#alerts) underneath.
2. **Top bar**: network status, notifications, voice search, inputs and settings.
3. **Favourites**: your pinned apps as large banners, ending with a **+ Add** tile.
4. **Recent**: apps you opened lately that aren't already in Favourites.
5. **Apps**: every app on the TV that can be opened, with a count next to the title.

Press **Home** or **Back** at any time to jump back to the first favourite.

## Opening and managing apps

Press **OK** on an app to open it.

To manage it, **hold OK** for a second or press the **Menu** (☰) button. A small menu opens next to
the app:

| Option | What it does | Shown for |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Opens the app | all |
| Add to Favourites / Remove from Favourites | Pins or unpins the app | all |
| Move | Lets you reposition it in Favourites | Favourites |
| Rename | Gives the app your own name in Lumen | all |
| Remove from Recent | Clears it from the Recent row | Recent |
| Hide from Apps | Removes it from the Apps grid | Apps |
| App info | Opens Android's page for the app (storage, permissions, force stop, updates) | all |
| Uninstall | Removes the app from the TV | apps you installed |

Built-in system apps can't be uninstalled from here; Android doesn't allow it.

Renaming only changes the name inside Lumen. To go back to the app's own name, choose **Rename** again
and select **Use original name**, or clear the text and save.

## Favourites

- **Pin an app:** use **+ Add** at the end of the row and pick from the list, or choose
  **Add to Favourites** from any app's menu.
- **Reorder:** open the app's menu, choose **Move**, then press ◀ ▶. The tile is outlined in blue while
  it's moving. Press **OK** or **Back** when it's where you want it. Up and Down are ignored while
  moving, so you can't lose your place.
- **Unpin:** choose **Remove from Favourites**.

On first launch Lumen pins the popular streaming apps it finds installed. If an app in Favourites is
uninstalled, it simply disappears from the row.

## Recent

Shows up to eight apps you opened from Lumen, newest first. Apps that are already favourites aren't
repeated. Use **Remove from Recent** to clear one, or turn the row off under
[Customize](#customizing).

Apps opened by other means (for example from a notification or a voice command) aren't counted.

## Hidden apps

**Hide from Apps** removes an app from the grid without uninstalling it. Hidden apps can still be
favourites.

To bring one back, select the **Hidden · N** chip at the right of the Apps title, or go to
**⚙ → Customize home screen → Hidden apps**, and pick the app.

## Top bar

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wi‑Fi / Ethernet / Offline | Current connection. Dimmed when offline |
| 🔔 Notifications | Badge with the number of notifications. Select it to see them; choose one to open it, or **Clear all**. Ongoing and background-service notifications are left out to keep the list useful. Needs [notification access](INSTALL.md#5-allow-notification-access-optional) |
| 🎤 Search | Starts the TV's voice search or assistant |
| ⇥ Inputs | Lists HDMI and AV inputs, with connected ones first and marked *connected*. Choose one to switch to it |
| ⚙ Settings | Customize the home screen, or jump to the TV's own settings pages |

The settings menu contains:

| Entry | Opens |
|---|---|
| Customize home screen | Lumen's own options (below) |
| Network & internet | Wi‑Fi and Ethernet settings. Shows the current state underneath |
| Picture | The TV's picture settings (falls back to Display & sound if the TV has no separate page) |
| Display & sound | Resolution, sound output and related settings |
| Remotes & accessories | Pairing remotes, gamepads and Bluetooth devices |
| Storage | Storage settings. Shows free space underneath |
| Apps | Android's app manager (update, clear data, permissions) |
| Date & time | Time zone and clock settings |
| All settings | The full Android settings app |

The Settings apps themselves aren't listed in the Apps grid, because the gear already leads there.

## Alerts

Alerts appear under the date only when something needs attention, and disappear on their own once it's
fixed. Select an alert to open the right settings page.

| Alert | Colour | When it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Not connected to a network | red | No Wi‑Fi or Ethernet connection |
| Wi‑Fi connected · No internet access | red | Connected, but Android can't reach the internet (router down, ISP outage, blocked DNS) |
| Wi‑Fi needs sign-in | amber | A hotel or office network is waiting for you to sign in |
| Storage running low · X free | amber | Less than 1 GB, or less than 10 %, of internal storage free |
| Storage almost full · X free | red | Less than 500 MB, or less than 5 %, free. Apps may fail to update or install |

Storage is checked every minute while the home screen is visible; network changes are picked up
immediately.

To free storage: **⚙ → Apps**, then clear the cache of large apps, or uninstall apps you don't use.

## Customizing

**⚙ → Customize home screen.** Each option changes the moment you press **OK** on it, so you can see
the result behind the menu. Press OK again to cycle to the next value, and **Back** to close.

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| Background | Midnight, Ocean, Aurora, Ember, Graphite, Black (pure black suits OLED TVs) |
| Apps per row | 4, 5 or 6 |
| App names | Always shown, or only on the selected app for a cleaner look |
| Sort apps | A to Z, Most used (counts launches from Lumen), Newest installed |
| Recent row | On or Off |
| Clock | System default, 12-hour or 24-hour |
| Hidden apps | Only shown when something is hidden |
| Reset home screen | Asks first, then restores the default favourites, unhides everything, clears custom names and launch history, and resets appearance |
